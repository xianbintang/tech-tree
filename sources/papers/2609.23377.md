## 摘要（原文）

This work targets the heterogeneous nature of repository-level software engineering tasks, where progress on some task categories can regress others under pooled reinforcement learning — a phenomenon the authors call the "category see-saw." We propose a category-aware expert training and policy-integration framework: repository-level tasks are organized into routable categories via a multi-axis, evidence-driven labeler (SWE Labeler); each category expert is iteratively trained with a Refresh-Repair-Expand (RRE) loop that alternates long-horizon Agentic RL with Repair SFT on self-generated trajectories; experts are then integrated into a single deployable policy via label-routed Multi-teacher On-Policy Distillation (MOPD) with ReLU-gated reward extrapolation. The resulting policy achieves 58.04% (from 52.64% baseline) average resolved rate on Pro-618 and 59.00% (from 56.22% baseline) on SWE-bench Multilingual.

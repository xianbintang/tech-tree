## 摘要（原文段落摘录）

"When you post-train a Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) model with Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF) or Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) at scale, three simultaneous challenges emerge... On AWS, you can address these challenges using Amazon Elastic Kubernetes Service (Amazon EKS), Elastic Fabric Adapter (EFA), and DeepEP... enabling DeepEP over EFA increased aggregate RL rollout throughput by 40 percent."
